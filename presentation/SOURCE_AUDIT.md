# Source audit

This deck uses the supplied ITMO PDF as a visual reference only. Claims and figures are grounded in the sources below.

| Deck claim or visual | Source | Use and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| First-year students with ADHD had lower mean course GPA than comparison students in fall and spring | Gormley et al. (2019), Table 2, https://doi.org/10.1177/1087054715623046 | Published group means and SDs are reproduced in the editable chart. This is observational evidence from U.S. first-year students; it is not Kazakhstan data and does not establish causation. |
| ACCESS improved outcomes for college students with ADHD | Anastopoulos et al. (2021), https://doi.org/10.1037/ccp0000553 | Randomized trial, N=250. ACCESS combined group CBT with individual mentoring; the trial does not isolate coaching alone. |
| Coaching and tutoring have different evidence patterns | DuPaul et al. (2017), https://doi.org/10.1111/ldrp.12143 | Five-year observational campus-service study. Coaching was associated with the greatest GPA gains for students with ADHD; this is not a randomized comparison. |
| Earlier and sustained disability-service use is associated with academic outcomes | Blasey et al. (2023), https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941221078011 | N=1,980 students registered with disability services across diagnoses. It is broad disability-service evidence, not ADHD-only evidence. |
| Higher-education intervention literature is heterogeneous | Alvarez-Godos et al. (2023), https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1216692; Ross et al. (2026), https://doi.org/10.1038/s44184-026-00196-4 | Reviews motivate comparing possible models; they do not prove that one model is best for Kazakhstan. |
| Research on disability support in higher education is mixed and broad | Römhild & Hollederer (2024), https://doi.org/10.1080/08856257.2023.2195074 | Scoping review across disability categories. Used to keep the proposal cautious about transferability. |
| Kazakhstan context includes existing disability support | Nazarbayev University, Disability and Special Learning Needs Support, https://nu.edu.kz/students/disability-and-special-learning-needs-support/ | Institutional example used to avoid claiming that Kazakhstan has no support at all. The proposal focuses on the need to examine ADHD-specific provision and evidence. |

No dataset-derived support-versus-no-support graph is included. The accompanying dataset audit found no sufficiently clear institutional academic-support variable, so manufacturing one would be invalid.
